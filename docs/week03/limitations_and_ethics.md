# Week 3 Limitations and Ethics Review

**Owner:** Nagireddy Nakka

## Limitations

1. Balanced windows do not represent natural attack prevalence.
2. The benchmark represents one vehicle environment.
3. CAN IDs may act as dataset-specific shortcuts.
4. Reservoir sampling preserves broad chronology but is not one untouched global contiguous split.
5. Normal windows come from legitimate frames within attack captures.
6. Recurrence construction averages payload bytes and can lose detail.
7. Strong future results will not prove real-vehicle safety.

## Ethical Position

- academic defensive prototype;
- human oversight required;
- no automatic vehicle control;
- no offensive CAN injection instructions;
- confidence must not be treated as certainty;
- false positives and false negatives must both be reported.
