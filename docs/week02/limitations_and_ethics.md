# Week 2 Limitations and Ethics Review

**Owner:** Nagireddy Nakka

## Dataset Limitations

1. The benchmark represents one vehicle environment.
2. Attack captures may contain source-specific CAN-ID patterns.
3. Legitimate traffic inside attack captures is used for Normal examples.
4. Raw attack prevalence is imbalanced.
5. Initial EDA does not establish cross-vehicle generalisation.
6. High later performance could reflect dataset-specific shortcuts.

## Ethical Risks for Later Weeks

- false confidence;
- missed attacks;
- unnecessary alarms;
- operator overreliance;
- dual-use publication;
- privacy risks in real telematics deployments;
- unsafe automated response.

## Week 2 Mitigations

- keep the notebook descriptive;
- disclose dataset limitations;
- avoid performance claims;
- exclude raw data from GitHub;
- preserve human oversight;
- record AI-tool assistance separately.
